Import from spreadsheet

Paste rows copied from the sheet (DATE, PR NUMBER, BUYER, SOLD ITEMS, QTY, UNIT PRICE, TOTAL, GRAND TOTAL, MOP). Rows without a PR number continue the receipt above. Grand totals are recalculated by the site. A PR that already exists is replaced.

How to encode a PR

Item Catalog
Items, categories, shorthand and prices

Shared: saved to the cloud, so every device uses the same items, categories, prices and shorthand (the last save wins). Saved PRs keep the price they were encoded with. Every item also gets automatic shorthand (initials, e.g. "gte" for Gospel Tracts English; "b7" for Book 7); the shorthand column lets you set your own. The category groups the item in the sales summary and the final report.

Price history

Every change to an item's price, newest first. Saved PRs keep the price they were encoded with.
